# Learning platforms
A curated collection of high-quality learning platforms for developing Tableau skills. Whether you're just getting started, preparing for interviews, or looking to deepen your expertise, these resources offer structured courses, guided learning paths, and hands-on exercises to help you become a more effective Tableau developer.

##
[**Dataford**](https://dataford.io/modules/tableau-interview-for-data-analysts)  
A structured interview preparation platform for aspiring and experienced data professionals. The Tableau module covers everything from core fundamentals (dimensions, measures, and calculations) to advanced topics like LOD expressions, parameters, sets, context filters, dashboard design, and performance optimization through hands-on, interview-focused lessons and practice questions. It's particularly valuable for preparing for Tableau-focused data analyst interviews while reinforcing practical product knowledge.
